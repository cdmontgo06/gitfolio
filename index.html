02 ASTROPHYSICS · HPC · JWST
RESEARCH

Seeing the Early Universe

Generating mock JWST observations of active galactic nuclei from cosmological simulations.

MOCK JWST IMAGE BLUETIDES / SYNTHESIZER / NIRCAM
SUMMER 2026 · CMU

As an undergraduate research intern, I worked on a computational astrophysics project comparing simulated high-redshift active galactic nuclei with observations from the James Webb Space Telescope.

The work involved BlueTides simulation data, the Vera supercomputer, Synthesizer, AGN emission models, spectral energy distributions, photometry, and mock NIRCam images.

As the project progressed, my work shifted from learning an existing pipeline to investigating parameter sensitivity, validating results, debugging the underlying software, and modifying the computational workflow itself.

ONE OF THE KEY LESSONS
A result is only as good as the assumptions it is built upon.

Investigating the emission grids revealed limitations in the original workflow and eventually led to identifying an issue in how AGN parameters were being passed through the model. I built a simplified test program, isolated the problem, and helped correct the full workflow.
