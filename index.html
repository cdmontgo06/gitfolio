Seeing the Universe
Before We Could

2026 RESEARCH
PROJECT IMAGE PLACEHOLDER MOCK JWST / AGN / NIRCam

Generating mock observations of high-redshift active galactic nuclei to understand how simulated galaxies might appear to JWST.

During my 2026 Summer Undergraduate Research Apprenticeship at Carnegie Mellon University, I worked with simulation data from BlueTides and Astrid to generate synthetic observations that could be compared with real James Webb Space Telescope data.

Working on CMU's Vera supercomputing cluster, I investigated how physical parameters in AGN emission models affected spectral energy distributions, photometry, and mock NIRCam observations.

Along the way, I worked directly with large HDF5 emission grids, debugged the mock observation pipeline, validated results numerically, and identified a parameter propagation issue that prevented several AGN parameters from affecting generated spectra.
